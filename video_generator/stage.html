Separation of Concerns: Math vs. AI ZERO HALLUCINATED NUMBERS
1. Python Owns All Currency Math: Revenue totals, variance checks, and 7-day pace multipliers are calculated deterministically before AI is called.
2. Strict Schema Validation: Gemini only synthesizes human-readable buying advice inside OpsBriefing (pydantic v2).
3. Automatic Deterministic Fallback: If GEMINI_API_KEY is absent or rate-limited, the rule-based engine generates the briefing without failing the nightly run.
